Questions

Does it replace my fiscal cash register?

No. POS For You handles orders, the kitchen and management. Official receipts still come from your registered cash register.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows computers, tablets and phones, in the browser or the Windows app.

What happens if the internet goes down?

You keep selling takeaway: sales are stored on the device and sent automatically when the connection returns. Sending table orders to the kitchen needs the connection.

Does it work with my printer?

With most 58 mm and 80 mm thermal receipt printers. Test yours during the free trial.

Is my data safe?

Data travels encrypted and every business only ever sees its own data. It is kept even if your subscription ends.
