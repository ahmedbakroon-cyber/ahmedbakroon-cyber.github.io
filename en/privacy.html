Privacy notice

What we store

Your business name, city, the owner's e-mail and phone, staff usernames, your menu, and the orders and payments recorded in the POS. Passwords and PINs are stored only as one-way hashes.

Why

Only to run the POS for your business (sales, kitchen, reports), to manage your subscription, and to contact you about your account. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where

On servers in Europe (London, United Kingdom). Each business can only see its own data.

How long

While your account exists. If you close it, we delete your business data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data at any time on WhatsApp.
